      Questions people ask us

      Do I need to download an app?

      No. Harvest-Link works inside WhatsApp. Save our number, say hello, and our assistant guides you step by step.

      Which languages can I use?

      Kannada, Hindi or English. You can type or send a voice note.

      What does it cost?

      Farmers pay a 2% fee on what they sell. Kirana stores pay a 2% service fee plus ₹3 per kg for delivery. You see every charge before you confirm.

      When and how do farmers get paid?

      Within 24 hours of pickup, straight to your bank account or UPI.

      Who picks up and delivers the vegetables?

      Our logistics partner collects produce from village pickup points, checks and weighs it, and delivers to stores before 7 AM.

      What if the vegetables are damaged or short?

      Produce is weighed and checked at pickup. If something arrives damaged or short, send a photo in the same chat and we sort it out.
